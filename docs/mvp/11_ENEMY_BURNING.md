# Enemy — Burning

**DESIGN STATUS: LOCKED**

## Fantasy

A Burning is the opposite of a Hollow.

Too much unresolved emotion remains.

Rage, pain, or panic burns through the manifestation from within until it is slowly destroying itself.

## Visual Identity

- medium/compact humanoid
- aggressive posture
- black/charred body
- violet cracks
- Death/Soul flame escaping through cracks
- claw-like arms are allowed
- much more visible violet than Hollow

Contrast:

- Hollow = cold, empty, slow
- Burning = unstable, overloaded, fast

## Behavior

Basic loop:

`Approach/Stalk → Stop → Violet Flare Telegraph → Charge → Hit or Miss → Recovery`

## Attack

One primary attack: **Charge**

The Burning explosively rushes toward the Player.

If it misses:

- short recovery
- creates a clear punish window

## Soul Sense

Do not show one stable Core.

Show multiple unstable **Soul Fractures** through the body.

This teaches that Soul Sense does not always reveal a single generic weakpoint.

## Soul Cannon Interaction

If the Soul Cannon hits a Burning while it is actively charging:

1. Soul Flame destabilizes.
2. Burning detonates.
3. Violet explosion damages nearby enemies.
4. Explosion does **not** damage the Player in the MVP.
5. Burning's actual Soul remains for normal release.

This is a skill reward, not the only way to kill Burning.

## Low Health Read

Suggested below ~25% HP:

- brighter flicker
- stronger cracks
- visibly unstable

A slight speed increase is allowed but **TUNABLE** and optional.

Do not add a new attack.

## Death

Normal kill:

- Burning falls/kneels briefly
- flames shrink
- manifestation breaks
- Soul appears

Detonation kill:

- violent explosion
- dark smoke/particles
- calm Soul remains floating in the center

The contrast is intentional.
