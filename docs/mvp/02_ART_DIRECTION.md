# Art Direction — Soulfire Gothic

**DESIGN STATUS: LOCKED**

## Direction

Working art-direction phrase:

> **SOULFIRE GOTHIC**

The world combines:

- Gothic architecture
- industrial machinery
- grotesque asymmetry
- Soul imagery
- dark anime energy
- modern VFX layered over pixel-art-inspired characters/world

## Palette

### World

Use primarily:

- black
- anthracite
- charcoal
- very dark desaturated violet
- gray
- silver
- dirty/off-white

### Flame of Death / Lost Soul Energy

Use:

- deep violet
- purple
- light violet
- near-white core

Purple is the primary supernatural color of the MVP.

### Flame of Life

Use:

- orange
- yellow
- white

Orange must be rare. Its rarity gives it meaning.

In the MVP, the strongest intentional use of orange is the Flame of Life at the ending.

## Environment

The world should suggest:

- cathedrals
- boilers
- furnaces
- oversized pipes
- giant bells
- chains
- Soul machinery
- industrial ruins
- strange symbols
- broken machinery
- asymmetrical Gothic arches

Avoid a clean modern factory.

Shapes may feel oversized, crooked, or subtly face-like without becoming comedy.

## Rendering Style

Preferred direction:

> Pixel-art-inspired characters and environment + smooth modern particles, glow, trails, distortion, screen effects, and camera effects.

Do not force all effects to a strict retro pixel grid.

## Silhouette Rule

Characters must remain readable from silhouette alone.

- Player — slender, long asymmetrical coat, large Scythe, heavy Cannon carried on back
- Hollow — tall and thin, white mask
- Burning — medium/compact, aggressive, cracked/flaming
- Devourer — large, wide, heavy

## Death Flame Rule

Death Flame must not behave like normal fire.

It may:

- move downward
- move sideways
- reverse direction
- split into sharp fragments
- briefly return toward its source
- form angular/geometric fragments

High-energy Death Flame may approach white at its core.

## Life Flame Rule

Life Flame is the visual opposite:

- soft
- organic
- warm
- upward movement
- stable
- calming

## Anime Impact

Anime feel should come primarily from:

- hitstop
- screen shake
- impact frames
- attack trails
- afterimages
- particles
- glow
- recoil
- strong audio

Do not require huge sprite animation counts.

## TARGET VISUAL

A cohesive dark Gothic-industrial arena where the Player and Souls are immediately readable because supernatural energy is the brightest visual element.

## PROTOTYPE FALLBACK

If finished assets do not exist:

- Player = dark humanoid/coat shape + purple chest core
- Scythe = dark handle + gray blade fragment + purple flame blade
- Cannon = large dark geometric weapon on back
- Hollow = tall dark humanoid + white oval mask
- Burning = dark humanoid + purple cracks/particles
- Devourer = large dark body + torso opening
- Souls = glowing white-purple shapes
- Arena = dark geometric Gothic-industrial primitives

Final art must never block gameplay implementation.
