# Arena — Abandoned Soul Furnace

**DESIGN STATUS: LOCKED**

## Purpose

The entire MVP takes place in one hand-built arena.

No procedural generation.

The arena must support:

- Scythe combat
- Ignition Dash
- Burning charges
- Cannon positioning
- Devourer Soul pressure
- mixed encounters
- Resonance payoff

## Theme

**Abandoned Soul Furnace**

An old Gothic-industrial facility once associated with Soul energy.

Visual ingredients:

- black stone
- dark metal
- oversized pipes
- crooked Gothic arches
- furnaces
- boilers
- chains
- broken Soul machinery
- floor cracks
- strange industrial/occult symbols
- asymmetrical structures

Purple environment lighting should remain restrained so gameplay Soul energy stands out.

## Scale

Approximately 1–1.5 screens of meaningful combat space, with camera follow.

Large enough for dashes and charges.

Small enough to avoid empty dead space.

## Combat Start

Suggested sequence:

1. Player enters/starts in arena.
2. brief calm
3. chest Core pulses
4. distant furnace/bell `DONG / WHUMM`
5. gates/arena state closes
6. Wave 1 starts

No tutorial UI is required.

**TUNABLE**

Adjust if necessary for pacing and fairness.

## Ending

After the final successful Soul Release:

1. enemies are gone
2. arena becomes quiet
3. violet effects calm/fade
4. a single small **orange Flame of Life** appears in the background
5. brief hold
6. show `THE LOST SOUL OF FIRE`
7. show `Prototype Complete`
8. allow restart

No cutscene engine.

## TARGET VISUAL

A readable dark Gothic-industrial arena with enough strange architecture to feel like a place rather than a collision test.

## PROTOTYPE FALLBACK

Use geometric primitives and simple textures to suggest:

- furnaces
- pipes
- arches
- chains
- machinery
- broken structures

Do not delay gameplay for detailed environment assets.
