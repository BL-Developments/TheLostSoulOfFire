# Player

**DESIGN STATUS: LOCKED**

## Fantasy

The Player is himself a Lost Soul who somehow carries the Flame of Death.

He should have left the world already.

The reason he did not is unknown in the MVP.

## Visual Identity

The Player is:

- slender
- medium-sized
- dark
- anime-reaper-inspired at a high level but original
- not a knight
- not heavily armored
- wearing a long asymmetrical coat or mantle
- messy/spiky-ish hair
- carrying a large Scythe
- carrying a physical Soul Cannon visibly on his back
- relaxed/cool in posture rather than constantly ultra-edgy

Palette:

- black
- anthracite
- very dark violet
- small silver/off-white details

The Player should be mostly a dark silhouette when idle.

Combat activates vivid violet-white energy.

## Death Flame Core

**LOCKED**

A Death Flame Core exists in the chest/heart area beneath the clothing.

Normally it is only faintly visible.

Universal energy-routing rule:

- Scythe: `CORE → ARM → SCYTHE`
- Soul Cannon: `CORE → SHOULDER → ARM → CANNON`
- Ignition Dash: `CORE → SPINE/LEGS → PROPULSION`
- Soul Sense: `CORE → NECK/HEAD → EYES`

At high Resonance:

- purple cracks/veins spread outward from the Core
- the Core becomes much brighter
- full Resonance mode outlines the silhouette with Death Flame

## Eyes

Normal:

- dark/neutral

Soul Sense:

- both eyes glow violet
- tiny flame trails are allowed

## Movement

Movement must feel:

- fast
- responsive
- direct
- near-immediate in acceleration

No stamina.

## Idle

Use subtle effects:

- slight coat movement
- faint Core pulse
- occasional 1–3 purple particles
- Scythe resting over shoulder or at side
- Soul Cannon visible on back

## Personality

No Player dialogue in MVP.

Behavioral presentation:

- calm
- controlled
- detached
- explosive only during combat actions

Target contrast:

> quiet → quiet → BOOM

## Taking Damage

On damage:

- white-purple flash
- Core flicker
- small hitstop
- small knockback
- readable audio cue

No gore focus.

## Death

Sequence:

1. HP reaches zero.
2. Brief freeze.
3. Purple cracks spread.
4. Body dissolves.
5. Flame of Death remains floating briefly.
6. Fade to black.
7. Retry prompt.

Respawn lore is not defined in the MVP.

## Resonance

During Resonance the Player becomes:

- visibly more powerful
- noticeably faster
- noticeably more mobile
- better at dashing
- stronger with Scythe
- faster/stronger with Soul Cannon

The mobility increase must be noticeable but not absurd or overpowered.

## PROTOTYPE FALLBACK

Use:

- dark humanoid silhouette
- simple coat-like shape
- white/off-white face marker if useful
- purple chest Core
- large Scythe
- large geometric Cannon on back

Mechanics and VFX must not wait for final character art.
