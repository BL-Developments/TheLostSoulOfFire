## The Two Flames

### Flame of Life

The Flame of Life binds **body and Soul**.

During natural death, the Flame of Life extinguishes and the Soul leaves the material world.

Visual language:

- orange
- yellow
- white core
- soft
- organic
- naturally rises upward
- warm and calm

### Flame of Death

The Flame of Death performs the opposite necessary function: **separation**.

It can sever an unnatural connection between a Lost Soul and the material world, allowing that Soul to finally leave.

It is not inherently evil.

Visual language:

- deep violet exterior
- light violet interior
- near-white core at high intensity
- sharp
- unstable
- partially geometric
- movement may go sideways, downward, backward, or otherwise behave "wrong"

## Lost Souls

Some Souls cannot move on.

Possible causes include:

- trauma
- violent death
- fear
- rage
- grief
- obsession
- hunger
- corruption
- overwhelming unresolved emotion

A trapped Soul distorts itself and sometimes the environment around it.

The hostile creature fought by the Player is a **manifestation** created by this failed transition.

When its HP reaches zero, the manifestation is destroyed. The actual Soul remains.

## The Player

The Player is also a Lost Soul.

He should already have passed on, but for an unknown reason he carries the Flame of Death.

This explains why he can:

- perceive Soul anatomy through Soul Sense
- release Lost Souls
- power the Soul Cannon
- accumulate Resonance
- enter an enhanced Death-Flame state

The exact origin of the Player and his Flame of Death remains a mystery in the MVP.

Do not explain it through dialogue, exposition, or a cutscene.

## Soul Residue

A successfully released Soul is **not consumed**.

After release, a small metaphysical residue remains. This residue is attracted to the Player's Flame of Death.

Flow:

`Lost Soul released → Soul leaves world → Soul Residue remains → Residue enters Player Core → Resonance increases`

## Emotional Manifestations

Lost Souls can physically reflect what keeps them trapped.

The MVP uses:

- **Hollow** — almost everything has burned out; emptiness remains
- **Burning** — emotion is so intense that the manifestation destroys itself
- **Devourer** — hunger/possession causes it to imprison other Souls

Future enemies may represent grief, obsession, fear, or other unresolved states, but no additional archetypes belong in the MVP.

## Environmental Distortion

Powerful Lost Souls may distort:

- architecture
- shadows
- object orientation
- scale
- geometry
- fire behavior
- spatial perception

The MVP arena should hint at this through asymmetry and unnatural Gothic-industrial construction without implementing a dynamic world-distortion system.

## Possible Future Lore

It may later be revealed that the Flame of Life and Flame of Death are different states of the same underlying force.

This is **not MVP canon that must be exposed to the Player**. Do not build mechanics or narrative around it yet.
