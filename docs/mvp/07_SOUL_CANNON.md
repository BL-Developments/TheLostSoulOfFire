# Soul Cannon

**DESIGN STATUS: LOCKED**

## Fantasy

The Soul Cannon is a real, physical, heavy **artificial Death-Flame Conduit**.

It cannot create the Flame of Death.

The Player feeds his own Flame of Death into the weapon.

This is why ordinary people cannot simply use it.

## Physical Presence

The Cannon must visibly exist even when inactive.

It is carried diagonally/on the Player's back with the visual weight of a large heavy weapon.

The intended silhouette should feel heavy and deliberate, but the design must remain original.

## Visual Identity

Gothic Industrial:

- black metal
- silver
- pipes
- runic/strange machinery details
- large barrel
- almost no purple while inactive

## Input

`RMB Hold → Charge`

`RMB Release → Fire`

## Use Sequence

1. Player draws Cannon from back.
2. Player braces it with both hands.
3. Flame routes `CORE → SHOULDER → ARM → CANNON`.
4. Purple energy enters chamber.
5. Charge grows.
6. Near-white compressed Core forms at high charge.
7. RMB release fires.
8. Strong recoil.
9. Cannon returns to back.

## Movement While Charging

Player may move while charging but is slower.

Do not fully root the Player.

## Charge

Continuous charge with three clearly readable visual/audio stages.

Suggested timing:

- 0.0–0.3 s: weak
- 0.3–0.8 s: strong
- 0.8–1.2 s: full

**TUNABLE**

Full charge must have an unmistakable cue.

## Full Shot

A full shot should feel extremely heavy.

Use:

- large violet-white projectile or beam
- strong recoil
- slight backward slide
- strong hitstop
- camera shake
- brief dark/black impact frame
- barrel flame explosion

Choose the simplest projectile/beam implementation that satisfies gameplay.

## Soul Sense Interaction

Soul Sense reveals valuable targets.

Full Cannon on Hollow Soul Core:

- massive stagger
- strong Core impact

Burning during charge:

- triggers special detonation

Devourer with consumed Souls:

- full Cannon can expel a trapped Soul

## Resonance Interaction

Same Cannon, no new mode.

During Resonance:

- faster charge
- larger shot
- increased damage
- stronger VFX

## Out of Scope

Do not add:

- ammunition
- reload
- overheat
- alternate firing modes
- Cannon upgrades
- multiple Cannons
